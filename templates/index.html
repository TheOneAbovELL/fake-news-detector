TruthLens

Check a news headline or article for warning signs. TruthLens combines a wording-based ML model with an optional AI fact-check assessment, and shows its reasoning so you can judge for yourself.

⚠ This server is running a demo model trained on synthetic data. Its scores are not meaningful.

TruthLens fetches the page and reads its headline and article text. Paywalled or JavaScript-only sites may not work; paste the text instead.

    How it works & limitations

    Wording signal (ML). A TF-IDF + logistic-regression classifier scores how much the wording resembles headlines that fact-checkers rated fake. It does not know facts. It was trained on a labelled headline dataset, mostly 2016–2018 US politics and celebrity news, so it is weakest on other topics, regions and languages.

    AI assessment (optional). When the server has a Groq API key, a large language model reviews the text for sourcing, plausibility and manipulation techniques. It cannot browse the web and can be wrong. Use its "claims to verify" as a starting point, not a final answer.

    • No verdict here proves that a story is true or false. Check important claims with primary sources and professional fact-checkers.
    • Your recent checks are stored only in your browser. The server doesn't keep the text or URLs you submit.
    • Text you check is sent to the AI provider (Groq) when the AI assessment is enabled.
